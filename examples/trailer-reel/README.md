# Termina trailer reel

The trailer of Termina at 9:16, for the phone: the same 10 scenes, times and music as
[`../trailer`](../trailer/), laid out again for a stage of 1080 x 1920. The scenes use the kit,
the fonts and the parts of the scenes of the trailer.

```powershell
termina\studio.cmd trailer-reel render --all
termina\studio.cmd trailer-reel export --mp4
```

`../trailer/music.py` writes the music of the two videos.
